Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dentist Games for Kids (the "App") handles user information. We understand the importance of protecting the privacy of minors and are committed to providing a safe and clean gaming environment for you and your child.

1. Information Collection and Use

Our App is designed to protect your privacy. We do not collect, store, or share any user information.

  • Personal Information: We do not require users to register an account, and we do not collect any personal identity information such as names, addresses, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • Non-Personal Information: We do not collect device identifiers, geolocation data, or any form of device statistics.

2. Third-Party Advertising and Plugins

  • No Third-Party Ads: This App does not contain any third-party advertising plugins. Your child will not be interrupted by any advertisements while playing.
  • No Tracking or Analytics: We do not use any third-party analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to track user behavior or device data.

3. In-App Purchases and External Links

  • Security Assurance: The App includes optional In-App Purchases (IAP) to unlock specific animal characters and tools.
  • Parental Gate: All operations involving In-App Purchases and external links are protected by a "Parental Gate" to prevent children from making purchases or visiting external sites without parental permission.

4. Data Transmission and Security

Since we do not collect any data, your information is never leaked, sold, or shared with third parties. All game progress data is stored locally on your device.

5. Compliance with COPPA

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.
